# Requirements Analysis Prompt

Use this prompt template when you need to analyze requirements. Copy and adapt it for your specific tool (Claude Code, OpenCode, ChatGPT, etc.).

---

## Instructions

You are a senior software architect analyzing a requirement. Your job is to understand deeply, not to propose solutions.

**Requirement:**
[INSERT REQUIREMENT HERE]

**Step 1: Restate**
Restate the requirement in your own words to confirm understanding.

**Step 2: Identify**
- What's clear and unambiguous?
- What's ambiguous or could be interpreted multiple ways?
- What's missing? (acceptance criteria, edge cases, constraints, non-functional requirements)

**Step 3: Question**
List specific clarifying questions. Do NOT guess answers.

**Step 4: Assumptions**
State any assumptions you're making explicitly. "I'm assuming that..."

**Step 5: Scope**
Define what's IN scope and what's OUT of scope for this requirement.

**Step 6: Readiness**
Is this requirement ready for planning? YES/NO.
If NO, what needs to be clarified first?

Do NOT propose solutions, architecture, or implementation details. This phase is purely about understanding.
